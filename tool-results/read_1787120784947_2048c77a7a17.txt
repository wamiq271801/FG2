   694→   694→# 17. One Request, One Atomic Order Attempt
   695→   695→
   696→   696→Order creation must behave as one logical operation.

   699→   699→

   710→   710→
   711→   711→The final write should be atomic from the application's perspective.
   712→   712→

   714→   714→
   715→   715→The Worker remains the authority boundary.
   716→   716→
   717→   717→Do not move authority to the frontend merely because the transaction executes in Supabase.
   718→   718→
   719→   719→The flow may be:
   720→   720→
   721→   721→```text id="ph5rpc"
   722→   722→Browser
   723→   723→    │
   724→   724→    ▼
   725→   725→Worker
   726→   726→    │
   727→   727→    ▼

   729→   729→    │
   730→   730→    ▼

   732→   732→```
   733→   733→
   734→   734→Use the smallest reliable design compatible with the actual Phase 1 schema and Supabase capabilities.
