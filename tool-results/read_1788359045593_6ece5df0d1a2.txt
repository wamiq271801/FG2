  1884→66. WHAT PRODUCT UPDATE MEANS
  1885→============================================================
  1886→
  1887→A product update invalidates the caches that depend on the changed product information.
  1888→
  1889→It does NOT automatically mean:
  1890→
  1891→invalidate homepage feed
  1892→
  1893→It does NOT automatically mean:
  1894→
  1895→invalidate every product list
  1896→
  1897→unless the actual cached data depends on the changed property.
  1898→
  1899→For example:
  1900→
  1901→Changing product description:
  1902→    → product data
  1903→
  1904→Changing category membership:
  1905→    → product data
  1906→    → old category listing
  1907→    → new category listing
  1908→    → other proven dependent scopes
  1909→
  1910→Changing active state:
  1911→    → product/listing visibility
  1912→    → sitemap / route-related data where applicable
  1913→    → feed only if actual feed membership depends on it
  1914→
  1915→Changing slug:
  1916→    → route identity
  1917→    → relevant product cache
  1918→    → SEO/sitemap dependencies
  1919→
  1920→Do not make a generic "invalidate everything" policy.
  1921→
