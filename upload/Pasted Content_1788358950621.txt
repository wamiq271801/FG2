============================================================
66. WHAT PRODUCT UPDATE MEANS
============================================================

A product update invalidates the caches that depend on the changed product information.

It does NOT automatically mean:

invalidate homepage feed

It does NOT automatically mean:

invalidate every product list

unless the actual cached data depends on the changed property.

For example:

Changing product description:
    → product data

Changing category membership:
    → product data
    → old category listing
    → new category listing
    → other proven dependent scopes

Changing active state:
    → product/listing visibility
    → sitemap / route-related data where applicable
    → feed only if actual feed membership depends on it

Changing slug:
    → route identity
    → relevant product cache
    → SEO/sitemap dependencies

Do not make a generic "invalidate everything" policy.

============================================================
67. WHAT FEED UPDATE MEANS
============================================================

A feed change means:

- product order changed
- product membership changed
- section membership changed
- feed ranking changed
- feed generation changed

Then:

feed:home

becomes stale.

Do NOT invalidate individual product caches simply because the feed changed.

============================================================
68. WHAT REVIEW UPDATE MEANS
============================================================

Review approval/rejection/update affects public approved review data.

Invalidate the appropriate review cache scope.

Do NOT invalidate product data merely because a review changed.

If the product page contains a review summary within a separate cached scope, tag that scope correctly during the cache redesign.
